1. Google Search
AI helps show the most relevant search results based on the query. Machine Learning analyzes search patterns, keywords, and user behavior to rank pages.

2. YouTube Recommendations
YouTube suggests videos that match interests and viewing history. ML models learn from watch time, likes, and searches to predict what users may want to watch next.

3. Google Maps
Google Maps provides traffic updates and the fastest routes. ML analyzes location data from millions of users to estimate traffic conditions and travel times.

4. Gmail Spam Filter
AI automatically detects and moves spam emails to the spam folder. ML learns from previously identified spam messages and email patterns.

5. Netflix Recommendations
Netflix recommends movies and TV shows based on viewing habits. ML studies watch history, ratings, and preferences to suggest relevant content.

6. Voice Assistants (Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa)
Voice assistants understand spoken commands and respond accordingly. ML uses speech recognition and natural language processing to interpret user requests.

7. Online Shopping Apps (Amazon, Flipkart)
Shopping platforms recommend products that users may like. ML analyzes browsing history, purchases, and product interactions to generate suggestions.

8. Face Unlock on Smartphones
AI recognizes a user's face to unlock the device. ML models learn facial features and compare them with stored facial data for authentication.

9. Social Media Feeds (Instagram, Facebook)
Social media platforms show posts that are most relevant to users. ML predicts content preferences based on likes, comments, shares, and viewing activity.

10. ChatGPT and AI Chatbots
AI chatbots answer questions and assist users in conversations. ML models are trained on large amounts of text data to understand language and generate responses.
